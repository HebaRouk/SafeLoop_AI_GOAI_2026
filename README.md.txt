# SafeLoop AI
## Multi-Agent School Safety & Incident Response System

### Track
**Boundless Agents — Topic 4: AI + Education**

---

## 1. Project Overview

SafeLoop AI is a multi-agent AI system designed to support school safety operations through intelligent incident intake, risk assessment, response planning, authorized remediation, independent verification, audit evidence, and continuous knowledge capture.

Instead of functioning as a generic chatbot, SafeLoop AI addresses a real operational workflow: transforming fragmented school safety events into a verified and auditable closed-loop resolution.

The system is designed for schools and educational organizations where incidents may originate from teachers, IoT sensors, maintenance systems, access-control systems, safety alerts, LMS/IT systems, and other authorized sources.

---

## 2. Problem

School safety incidents can arrive through multiple disconnected channels.

Examples include:

- Teacher safety reports
- IoT sensor alerts
- Maintenance requests
- Access-control alerts
- Safety system notifications
- IT/LMS incidents
- Parent-reported concerns

These events may be duplicated, incomplete, or difficult to prioritize.

A school safety team needs to answer several questions quickly:

1. What happened?
2. How serious is the incident?
3. What evidence supports the assessment?
4. What could be causing the incident?
5. What response should be taken?
6. Does the response require human approval?
7. Was the action actually successful?
8. What evidence proves the outcome?
9. What can be learned for future incidents?

SafeLoop AI addresses this workflow through specialized AI agents.

---

## 3. Target Users

Primary users include:

- School safety officers
- School administrators
- Teachers
- Maintenance teams
- Security teams
- IT/LMS support teams
- Educational organization operations teams

The system is designed to assist authorized personnel rather than replace human responsibility for high-risk safety decisions.

---

## 4. Core Innovation

The core innovation of SafeLoop AI is a closed-loop multi-agent architecture.

The workflow is:

```text
Incident
   ↓
Intake & Deduplication
   ↓
Risk Assessment
   ↓
Root Cause Analysis
   ↓
Response Planning
   ↓
Human Approval (when required)
   ↓
Authorized Remediation
   ↓
Independent Verification
   ↓
Evidence & Audit
   ↓
Knowledge Capture
